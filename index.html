Expansiveness

I. Human, but more. We currently have access to only a fraction of what our minds are capable of.

Human minds are bigger than they seem. They can contain a million songs, the smell of the moon, abstract symbols.

We were forged in an animal world; like all our forebears, in a formless expanse, of leaves and shades of green. From here, we've conceived of inconceivable structure. Once, twice, over and over. We've created Euclidean geometry. Classical music. The idea of computing. How could you not love this species?

Consider the following:

  1. Our ability to think is circumscribed by our ability to communicate. Language was the first radical communication technology, and has so drastically changed us as to enable complex civilization.
  2. We are not at the limits of possible communication. Increasing communication will lead to an explosion of thought, creation, and understanding.

In every human, there is the capacity to be all humans.

To reach a state where every human can manifest their highest desires, we need telepathy. The safe transmission of ideas and abilities wholesale is patently lacking.

Telepathy consists in two things: the mapping of our ideas, intentions, and mental activities to the correct external action, where the "action" captures the full fidelity of the idea (mind-to-world), and the mapping of those ideas in full fidelity to another person's mind (mind-to-mind). We'll start with the mind-to-world interface, and then build a mind-to-mind interface. Our mind-to-world interface will enable astonishing experiences:

In this regime, ordering an Uber is as primitive in experience as moving your index finger; the distance between asking a question and receiving an answer approaches the speed at which you can access your own memories; the connection between you and your tools is so seamless that it is as if you can hold a whole codebase, genome, dataset, company, etc. in your head.
